I write about life in
the in-between spaces,

Between the heavens and hell,
Between fear and freedom,
Between the crazy and the crazy awesome,
Between the idea and the reality,
Between the stranger and the friend,
Between the mess and the masterpiece,
Between the leap and the landing,

For this is where beauty lies.

(and also other ramblings about this and other realities)
